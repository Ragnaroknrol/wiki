<Infobox variant="ice" title="How it works">
Badges sit on a fan's public profile. Hover one and its tooltip appears: the badge's name, and a
line from Le Council about what you did to earn it. The [L.A.M.P.](/guides/idols-and-lamp) lights
when a badge is granted.
</Infobox>
