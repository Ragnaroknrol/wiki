<Infobox variant="ice" title="Since Season 3, kept for Season 4">
Every UTC weekday carries a rule that applies to every game played that day, league-wide. Each
one has a public name, an [LPCHUIP advisory](/modifiers/advisories), and a measured correction to
the [odds](/guides/economy#odds). Season 04 keeps all seven. It opens on a Monday, so for the
first twenty-four games the buildings are only buildings. The first Second Chance Sunday of
Season 04 is **25 October 2026**.
</Infobox>

Calendar rules sit on top of the three [modifier](/modifiers/) tiers. They are not per-game
conditions—they are the day itself, and they apply to all twenty-four games on the slate.

| Day | Rule | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| **Sunday** | 🔁 **Second Chance Sundays** | A game sitting at a one-goal margin at the horn is levelled and plays on. Roughly 15% of games are levelled, which doubles the overtime rate from 15% to 30%. |
| **Monday** | 🔇 **Muted Mondays** | [Arena modifiers](/modifiers/arena) are suppressed. No home-ice boost. Every building is just a building. |
| **Tuesday** | 🌌 **Aurora Tuesdays** | [Player modifiers](/modifiers/player) amplified ×1.25, plus a bonus on shots on goal. |
| **Wednesday** | 🌀 **Weird Wednesdays** | The home side plays under the *visitor's* arena condition. Two [match modifiers](/modifiers/match) instead of one. |
| **Thursday** | 🥅 **Thankless Thursdays** | Backup goalies start. No pulls, no swaps. |
| **Friday** | 🥊 **Fist-Fight Fridays** | Fights rise from about 1.70 a game to 4.15. Penalty minutes go from roughly 28 to 52. |
| **Saturday** | ⑥ **Sixth Saturdays** | Returns from [the Sixth](/lore/the-sixth) shift to an even 30/30/30 gain, lose, or swap—and a repeat visit guarantees a change. |

## The one that changes results

**Second Chance Sundays is the only rule that touches a result directly.** Every other rule
changes the conditions a game is played under; Sunday reaches into a finished game, unfinishes
it, and makes them keep going.

Le Council has been asked how a game that has ended can be levelled. Le Council has recorded the
question.

## The Last Mile

One more day is different, and it comes once a season rather than once a week. The **last
matchday of the regular season** is played under a [match modifier](/modifiers/match#the-last-mile)
on every game: standings points count twice, payouts do not. It is not a calendar rule, and it
sits on top of whatever the weekday already does.

## Reading the odds against the day

[O.D.D.S.](/lore/organizations#oddsoffice-of-data-driven-speculation) applies a measured
correction for the day of the week. So does the market. A Thursday line that looks wrong for a
team may simply be its backup goalie.
