A Hockay season is short and relentless. The league never sleeps at all—Season 04 runs a game at
the top of **every hour, around the clock**, from D01 to D46. It opens at **00:00 UITC on Monday
19 October 2026**.

<Infobox variant="neutral" title="Season lengths are not fixed">
S01 ran 38 matchdays. S02 ran 46. S03 ran 46, and S04 runs 46. [Le Council](/lore/lpchuip) sets the schedule and
does not commit to a number in advance.
</Infobox>

## Regular season—46 matchdays

Since the [conference split](/lore/conferences), a team plays a **round-robin inside its own
conference**: each of its twenty-three conference rivals once at home and once away. That is 46
games per team.

- **24 games per matchday**—all 48 teams play, every day.
- **One game at the top of every hour**, starting at **00:00 UITC**. The slate never stops and
  there is no dead window. ([The Ice does not recognize timezones and Le Council does not enjoy
  them.](/guides/faq#what-does-uitc-stand-for-again))
- **1,104 games** across the regular season.
- Every team is at home for exactly half its games.

Cross-conference games are labelled **Inter**. They count for points like any other.

### Single-game structure

- **Period 1**—~15 minutes of events
- **Intermission**—~5 minutes ([The Jambono](/lore/the-jambono) resurfaces; the
  [shavings](/guides/ice-shavings) accumulate)
- **Period 2**—~15 minutes of events
- **Intermission**—~5 minutes
- **Period 3**—~15 minutes of events
- **Post-game wrap**—final stats, [JM](/lore/commentators) commentary
- **Overtime / shootout** if tied

From Season 04, [G.L.A.S.S.](/guides/simulation#glass-can-see-them-now) draws every player on the
ice as the game airs.

### Points

Two points for a win. One point for an overtime or shootout loss. Zero for a regulation loss.
Le Council trusts you can do the arithmetic.

Each conference keeps its own table, positions 1–24.

### The day of the week matters

Every UTC weekday carries a league-wide rule that applies to all twenty-four games on the slate—
suppressed arena modifiers on Monday, backup goalies on Thursday, four fights a game on Friday.
See [calendar rules](/guides/calendar-rules).

### The last day

The final matchday is not like the others. In Seasons 1 and 2 every game on it ran under
[Triple or Nothing](/modifiers/match#triple-or-nothing). In Season 3 it ran under
[The Last Mile](/modifiers/match#the-last-mile): standings points count twice, payouts do not.
Le Council forgot this in its own arithmetic: it wrote that Nairobi could not be caught with three
matchdays worth six points, when the three were worth eight. Oslo took all eight and finished
first in the Givre by a single point.

## Playoffs

Seasons 1 and 2 sent eight teams into a single bracket. Since Season 3, **each conference runs
its own bracket**, and the two winners meet in one Final. Sixteen teams make the bracket.
Eighteen skate.

### The Wildcard

- Seeds **1–7 clinch** outright. No draw applies to them.
- The **8th seed does not advance. It hosts.** Its opponent is **drawn** from every team that
  finished 9th through 24th in the conference—sixteen teams, one draw, one seat.
- The draw is weighted toward the higher finish: the 9th holds *"nearly thirty percent"* of it,
  the 24th *"less than two."* Finishing well is rewarded. Finishing poorly is not disqualifying.
- The draw is conducted once per conference. It is not repeated and it is not revisited.
- The Wildcard is **one game**. The winner takes the 8th seed into the quarterfinals.
- The loser is recorded as having lost the Wildcard—*"a different thing from failing to qualify,
  and a better thing to have on the record."*

### The bracket

| Round | Format |
|---|---|
| **Quarterfinals** | Best-of-three. 1 v 8, 4 v 5, 2 v 7, 3 v 6. |
| **Semifinals** | Best-of-three. The 1–8 and 4–5 winners meet; so do the 2–7 and 3–6 winners. |
| **Conference Final** | Best-of-three. One winner per conference. |
| **The Final** | One game. Verglas against Givre. |

Standing bets on making the playoffs settle on the Quarterfinal field, not on the table.

The bracket is fixed. Nobody is reseeded. The higher seed hosts games one and three of a series.
Teams from opposite [conferences](/lore/conferences) can only meet in the Final.

Each round is a run of consecutive nights, starting at 20:00 UITC and filling one hour per game.
The quarterfinals start at 16:00, because eight games from 20:00 would cross midnight and a night
that spans two days is not a matchday.

### The Final

**One game. Special format.** Each period lasts **one full hour**—three to four hours in total
with intermissions. It is the only match in the season played at this pacing.

[The Puck](/lore/the-puck) has more time here. Strange things happen.

**Since Season 3 the Final is played on neutral ice.** The petition *Neutral Combined Site for
Finals Game*, filed by **Assassin66** and carried at 81 of 81 Seals, was enacted on 1 September
2026: the Final is played on neither finalist's ice, *"under the combined effects of both their
buildings."* Le Council ruled the same day that in twenty seasons there had never been a patch
of neutral ice. On 28 September there was one: Graine Wetsky Funds opened
[Graine Wetsky Arena](/lore/graine-wetsky-arena), a sheet poured from recovered shavings, and gave
it as the venue at no cost. The petition left Le Council no power to decline it. The petition did
not ask for that building. It is the only one there is.

After the Final, [The Puck speaks](/lore/the-puck#the-annual-sentence)—a single sentence,
delivered enciphered. The community panics either way.

### Rest Day

No games. No announcements. No advisories. Silence.
[Le Council recommends sitting with whatever just happened.](/guides/faq#what-is-rest-day)

## The intersaison

Then the season is over and the machinery starts moving. Season 03's intersaison ran in this
order:

- **The Tribunal convenes** the day after Rest Day, at 00:00 UITC.
  **[The S.H.I.F.T.](/guides/shift)** opens in two rounds of three days. Front offices file,
  shareholders vote, the Tribunal registers what survives.
- **Dividends.** [S.L.U.S.H.](/guides/exchange) pays the season dividend on every share, weighted
  by how far each team went, on holdings **as they stand at the minute the Tribunal convenes**.
  A share bought after that minute does not collect.
- **The All-Star games.** Two exhibition games, on the second and third nights after the Final,
  between the **All-Stars Verglas** and the **All-Stars Givre**. From Season 03, **each team
  sends one player at most**, so a conference of twenty-four fills fifteen seats and nine teams
  send nobody. Skaters are chosen on regular-season points, goaltenders on save percentage, and
  the host buildings are drawn. The games touch no standings, bracket, or team record, but the
  books take wagers on them.
- **Ratings.** The finished season goes into the players' ratings. After Season 03, 360 players
  rose, 310 fell, and fifty did not play enough to be measured.
- **[The Resurfacing](/lore/the-jambono#between-seasons-the-resurfacing).** The Jambono goes to
  work between seasons. Things are different afterward. We apologize.
- **The Eve.** The day before D01. The rosters go public, the schedule lands, Series ∞ posts its
  first round of new shares on [the Exchange](/guides/exchange), and La Confrérie strikes the new
  season's [cards](/guides/cards). For Season 04, The Eve also brought in the **ten percent
  ceiling** on every team but your own.
- **Expansion, when it happens.** The twenty-four Season 3 teams all manifested during a single
  intersaison. [Le Council does not control this.](/lore/almighty-ice)

Then D01 again, at 00:00 UITC.

**The Final**, 10 October, was the first game Graine Wetsky Arena ever held. Nairobi led 5-3 after
the first hour; it was 9-9 after the second. **Nyambura Kamau** tied it 12-12 on the power play
with one second left in regulation. **Curtis Favel**, a Winnipeg defender, won it twenty-one
minutes and six seconds into overtime, assisted by Tyler Chicken. Nairobi's **Brian Kipchoge**
had four goals and two assists, more points than anyone in the building, and lost. Winnipeg went
7-2 in the postseason, and Favel led it with 14 points. Every one of the 3,458 kcups the building
recovered that night went to the Funds.
